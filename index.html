Why AI projects stall

Most enterprise AI never gets past the pilot.

The model is rarely the problem. The hard part is everything around it: your data rules, your systems and the people who have to trust the output. That is the work we do.

  1. Your data can't go to a public AI service

    Customer records, drawings, pricing and banking data are bound by secrecy rules, PDPA and client contracts. Sending them to a third-party API is not an option.

  2. Generic chatbots don't know your systems

    Useful AI has to read from your ERP, MES, core banking and document stores, and act inside them safely. Off-the-shelf tools stop at the chat window.

  3. Nobody can answer to the auditor

    Who used the AI, what it saw, what it did and who approved it. Without those answers, risk and compliance teams will say no, and they should.

Agents

Custom AI agents

Agents that do a defined job: review a loan file, draft a QC report, plan maintenance, answer policy questions with sources. Each one has clear permissions and hands off to a person when it should.

MCP

MCP server development

We build Model Context Protocol servers that give AI controlled, logged access to your systems, such as SAP, MES, SQL databases, SharePoint and internal APIs. Read-only by default, with write access only where you approve it.

LLM

On-premise LLM deployment

We size the hardware, choose and tune open-weight models, and install the inference stack in your data centre or private cloud. Then we hand it over to your IT team with runbooks, monitoring and support.

Governance

AI governance & security

Access control, audit trails, evaluation and approval steps built into the system from the start, and documentation your risk, compliance and audit teams can sign off.

On-premise AI

Your AI stack, inside your perimeter.

Everything runs on infrastructure you own or control. Models, prompts, documents, embeddings and logs stay on your network. If your policy requires it, the whole system runs fully air-gapped.

  • Model choice. Open-weight models such as Llama, Qwen, Mistral and DeepSeek, chosen and tested for your language, documents and tasks.
  • Hardware sizing. From a single GPU workstation for a department to a multi-node cluster, sized to your users and response-time targets.
  • Private knowledge. Retrieval over your own documents with access rules carried through, so people only see answers from files they may open.
  • Operated by you. We hand over with runbooks and training. Ongoing support and model updates are available if you want them.

AI governance

Controls your risk team will sign off.

Governance is part of the design, not a policy document written after launch. Every system we deliver includes these controls.

Identity & access

Single sign-on with your directory. Agents inherit each user's permissions, so AI never becomes a way around your access rules.

Complete audit trail

Every prompt, retrieved document, tool call and response is logged with the user and time, and stored on your servers.

Human approval

Actions that change records, send messages or move money wait for a named person to approve them.

Data residency

Data stays in the country and the building you choose. Nothing is used to train anyone else's model.

Evaluation & testing

Accuracy tests on your own cases before go-live, plus checks for prompt injection and data leakage, repeated when models change.

Regulatory alignment

Documentation mapped to the rules you work under, such as PDPA, BNM RMiT, Hong Kong PDPO and ISO/IEC 27001.

How we work

From first conversation to a system your team runs.

A typical engagement moves through four stages. We agree scope and success measures at each one, so you can stop or change course between them.

  1. 1

    Typically 2 weeks

    Discover

    Our engineers sit with the people doing the work, map the process and data, and pick the one or two use cases with the clearest return.

  2. 2

    Typically 4 to 6 weeks

    Prototype

    We build a working agent on your real data, in a secure environment, and measure it against the success criteria we agreed.

  3. 3

    Typically 4 to 8 weeks

    Deploy

    We install the model and agents on your infrastructure, connect them through MCP servers, harden security and train your users.

  4. 4

    Ongoing

    Operate & extend

    Your team runs it with our runbooks. We stay available for support, model updates and the next use case.

Carbon reporting with AI

Verification is in our name.

A carbon credit is only worth what can be proven about it. Verificer uses the same engineering we bring to banks and factories to measure, report and evidence carbon, so every tonne claimed can be traced back to its source data.

We prepare greenhouse gas reports and carbon project documentation that follow the Verra Verified Carbon Standard (VCS) and ISO 14064, ready for review by an independent validation and verification body.

Our flagship project

Keeping a forest reserve standing, and proving it.

We are developing a carbon project on a forest reserve, where protecting the trees generates carbon credits. AI does the heavy lifting: it compares satellite images over time to watch the canopy, flags possible clearing early, and turns field measurements into biomass and carbon estimates. Our engineers then assemble the evidence into a Verra project document that an auditor can check line by line.

  1. M

    Measure

    Satellite and drone imagery, field plot data, fuel and electricity records. AI reads invoices, meter data and utility bills so nobody has to retype them.

  2. R

    Report

    Calculations follow the chosen Verra methodology or ISO 14064-1 for organisational Scope 1, 2 and 3 inventories, with every emission factor and assumption recorded.

  3. V

    Verify

    An audit-ready data room for the verification body under ISO 14064-3. Every number in the report opens to the document or dataset behind it.
